About BAANA

Preserving Heritage, Building Community

Who We Are

BAANA (Bhojpuri Awadhi Association of North America) is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization [Tax Id - 83-1541155] committed to presenting and promoting the language, arts, culture, and traditions of the Indian diaspora from the regions of Awadh, Purvanchal, Bhojpur, Mithila, Magadh, and Santhal.

We are dedicated to the preservation, promotion, and enhancement of our languages, cultures, arts, and traditions from the heartland of Bihar-Uttar Pradesh in the USA. Our organization serves as a vibrant platform for communities across North America to connect, celebrate, and preserve their rich cultural heritage.

🎯

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a vibrant platform for our communities across North America to connect and celebrate their heritage. We strive to advance the recognition of these languages both locally and globally by:

  • ✓ Establishing educational programs
  • ✓ Advocating for official language status
  • ✓ Fostering growth of regional arts and media
  • ✓ Developing language resources
  • ✓ Creating cultural projects and initiatives
👁️

Our Vision

We envision a future where the languages, arts, and traditions of Bihar-Uttar Pradesh are celebrated and preserved for generations to come. Our vision includes:

  • ✓ Global recognition of our languages
  • ✓ Thriving cultural communities across North America
  • ✓ Educational opportunities for all ages
  • ✓ Strong media presence and representation
  • ✓ Enriched cultural tapestry of North America

What We Do

Our initiatives and programs to preserve and promote our cultural heritage

🎉

Cultural Events

We organize and celebrate major festivals including Holi, Bihar Diwas, Makarsankranti, Hartalika Teej, and our Annual Gala Event, bringing our community together.

📚

Educational Programs

We develop and support educational initiatives to teach our languages, history, and cultural traditions to younger generations.

🤝

Community Services

Through various projects and activities, we work collaboratively to support and uplift our community, fostering a spirit of generosity and shared responsibility.

🎭

Arts & Media

We promote regional arts, music, dance, and media platforms to showcase our rich cultural heritage on a global stage.

🌐

Language Advocacy

We advocate for the official recognition and preservation of Bhojpuri and Awadhi languages at local and international levels.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Engagement

We create opportunities for families to connect, share experiences, and build lasting relationships within our community.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

🏛️ Cultural Preservation

We are committed to preserving and passing on our centuries-old traditions, languages, and cultural practices to future generations.

🤲 Community Unity

We believe in the power of community and work to bring people together, fostering connections and shared experiences.

📖 Education & Growth

We prioritize education and continuous learning, providing resources and opportunities for personal and cultural growth.

🌟 Excellence

We strive for excellence in all our programs and initiatives, ensuring quality and meaningful impact in everything we do.

🌍 Inclusivity
