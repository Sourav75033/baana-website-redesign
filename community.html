Community Engagement

Celebrating Our Rich Cultural Heritage Together

Our Cultural Celebrations

BAANA brings our community together through vibrant cultural celebrations, festivals, and events that honor our traditions and strengthen our bonds. Join us in celebrating the rich heritage of Bihar-Uttar Pradesh.

Major Festivals

Makarsankranti

A lively festival that celebrates the sun's entry into Capricorn. It is marked by vibrant festivities, including kite flying, bonfires, with Dahi-Chura and Tilkut sweets, symbolizing the end of winter and the start of the harvest season.

Traditional Activities:
  • 🪁 Kite flying competitions
  • 🔥 Community bonfires
  • 🍚 Dahi-Chura and Tilkut sweets
  • 🎵 Traditional songs and dances

Holi

A vibrant celebration of colors, unity, and joy. The festival, known for its lively street festivities, sees locals and visitors alike coming together to throw colorful powders, dance, and enjoy traditional sweets, reflecting the city's rich cultural heritage and the arrival of spring.

Celebration Highlights:
  • 🎨 Colorful powder throwing
  • 💃 Traditional folk dances
  • 🍬 Delicious traditional sweets
  • 🎶 Live music and entertainment

Bihar Diwas

Observed every year on March 22, marking the formation day of the state of Bihar. A special day for our community to come together and celebrate the cultural, economic, and social progress of Biharis across the globe.

Event Features:
  • 🎤 Cultural performances
  • 🏆 Recognition of community achievements
  • 🍛 Traditional Bihari cuisine
  • 📚 Educational programs for youth

Hartalika Teej

Celebrated for three days in the month of Bhadrapada according to Hindu calendar. Married women and unmarried girls will pray to Teej Mata, Goddess Parvati. Women observe a waterless fast on these three days and worship the Goddess.

Rituals & Traditions:
  • 🙏 Prayer ceremonies to Goddess Parvati
  • 💚 Traditional green attire
  • 🎨 Mehndi application
  • 🎵 Devotional songs and gatherings

About BAANA
