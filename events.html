Annual Events

Regular celebrations throughout the year

Makarsankranti

📅 January (Annual)

Celebrate the harvest season with kite flying, bonfires, traditional sweets like Dahi-Chura and Tilkut, and community gatherings. A joyous festival marking the end of winter.

Holi

📅 March (Annual)

The festival of colors! Join us for colorful powder throwing, traditional music and dance, delicious sweets, and community bonding. A celebration of spring and unity.

Bihar Diwas

📅 March 22 (Annual)

Commemorate Bihar's formation day with cultural performances, traditional cuisine, community awards, and celebrations of our rich heritage and achievements.

Hartalika Teej

📅 September (Annual)

A three-day celebration for women, featuring prayers to Goddess Parvati, traditional rituals, mehndi application, devotional songs, and cultural gatherings.

About BAANA

BAANA is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization [Tax Id - 83-1541155] committed to present and promote the language, arts, culture and traditions of Indian diaspora from the regions of Awadh, Purvanchal, Bhojpur, Mithila, Magadh, and Santhal regions.
