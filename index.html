Our Mission

We are dedicated to the preservation, promotion, and enhancement of our languages, cultures, arts and traditions from the heartland of Bihar-Uttar Pradesh in the USA.

Our mission is to provide a vibrant platform for our communities across North America to connect and celebrate their heritage. We strive to advance the recognition of these languages both locally and globally by establishing educational programs, advocating for official status, and fostering the growth of regional arts and media on a global stage.

Through our initiatives, including the development of language resources, media platforms, and cultural projects, we aim to enrich the cultural tapestry of North America and ensure the continued legacy of our centuries-old traditions.

Community Highlights

Celebrating our rich cultural traditions and festivals

Makarsankranti

A lively festival celebrating the sun's entry into Capricorn. Marked by vibrant festivities including kite flying, bonfires, with Dahi-Chura and Tilkut sweets, symbolizing the end of winter and the start of the harvest season.

Holi

A vibrant celebration of colors, unity, and joy. The festival, known for its lively street festivities, sees locals and visitors alike coming together to throw colorful powders, dance, and enjoy traditional sweets.

Hartalika Teej

Celebrated for three days in the month of Bhadrapada according to Hindu calendar. Married women and unmarried girls will pray to Teej Mata, Goddess Parvati. Women observe a waterless fast on these three days.

About BAANA
