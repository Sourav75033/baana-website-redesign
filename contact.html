Frequently Asked Questions

How can I become a member?

Simply fill out the contact form above with "Membership Inquiry" as the subject, or email us directly at info@baanausa.org. We'll send you all the information about membership benefits and the registration process.

How do I register for events?

Visit our Events Calendar page to see upcoming events. Each event has registration information and links. You can also contact us directly for assistance with event registration.

Can I volunteer for BAANA events?

Absolutely! We always welcome volunteers. Select "Volunteer" in the contact form subject, or reach out to us directly. We'll connect you with volunteer opportunities that match your interests and availability.

How can my business sponsor BAANA?

We offer various sponsorship levels for businesses and individuals. Contact us with "Sponsorship Opportunities" as the subject, and we'll provide detailed information about sponsorship packages and benefits.

How do I join the Facebook or WhatsApp groups?

Contact us through the form above or email us directly, and we'll send you the links to join our active Facebook group (130+ members) and WhatsApp group (106+ members).

About BAANA

BAANA is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization [Tax Id - 83-1541155] committed to present and promote the language, arts, culture and traditions of Indian diaspora from the regions of Awadh, Purvanchal, Bhojpur, Mithila, Magadh, and Santhal regions.
