About BAANA

BAANA is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization [Tax Id - 83-1541155] committed to present and promote the language, arts, culture and traditions of Indian diaspora from the regions of Awadh, Purvanchal, Bhojpur, Mithila, Magadh, and Santhal regions.
